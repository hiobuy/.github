# HioBuy Developer Platform

Build applications with **Taobao, 1688, AI product discovery, China sourcing, procurement, warehouse fulfillment, and international shipping APIs**.

HioBuy provides developer infrastructure for teams building cross-border commerce, sourcing platforms, fulfillment systems, and AI-powered commerce applications connected to China's e-commerce ecosystem.

Explore HioBuy through small, practical, open-source examples that are easy to clone, understand, modify, and deploy.

## 🛠 What You Can Build

HioBuy APIs provide infrastructure for building a wide range of commerce and sourcing applications.

### Product Discovery

Search and retrieve products, SKUs, pricing, images, variants, and product information from supported Chinese marketplaces.

### AI Sourcing Applications

Build AI product finders, sourcing assistants, shopping agents, Telegram or Discord bots, and other AI-powered commerce experiences using HioBuy Agent APIs and MCP.

### Procurement

Create sourcing and purchasing workflows for products from China.

### Warehouse Fulfillment

Receive packages at HioBuy warehouses, manage inbound packages, request value-added services, consolidate shipments, and prepare orders for international delivery.

### International Shipping

Discover available shipping channels, estimate freight, create shipments, pay shipping charges, and retrieve shipment tracking information.

### AI Agents & MCP

Give AI agents access to HioBuy commerce capabilities through Agent APIs and MCP-compatible tools.

Combine product discovery, sourcing, procurement, fulfillment, and logistics capabilities with your own AI applications.
